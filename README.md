# Aerospace Engineering Portfolio

I'm Matthew Dula, a Mechanical Engineering student at Case Western Reserve University with interests in aerospace engineering, spacecraft dynamics, controls, and GNC.

This repository contains engineering projects, simulations, and technical work involving aerospace dynamics, numerical methods, control systems, rocketry, CAD, and fabrication. I use it both to document completed work and to preserve the analysis, assumptions, and development behind ongoing projects.

## Current Work

### Spacecraft Control Systems Research
Undergraduate research focused on the modeling and control of spacecraft operating in nonlinear dynamical environments.

Topics include:
- Nonlinear spacecraft dynamics and numerical simulation
- Linearization and state-space modeling
- Feedback and sampled-data control
- Event-triggered control architectures
- Stability and performance analysis

### High-Power Rocketry
Design, fabrication, simulation, and testing through CWRU Rocketry, including work toward an L1 high-power rocket.

Areas of work include:
- Rocket design and flight simulation
- Structural fabrication
- Recovery-system design
- CAD and digital fabrication

## Selected Projects

### Rocket Flight Dynamics & Monte Carlo Simulation
A physics-based Python simulation for analyzing model-rocket flight dynamics and sensitivity to uncertain parameters.

The project includes:
- Numerical integration of the equations of motion
- Time-varying mass and motor thrust data
- Aerodynamic drag and gravitational effects
- Monte Carlo and Latin Hypercube sensitivity analysis
- Numerical convergence analysis

### Model Rocket Launch Safety Analysis
An analysis combining OpenRocket simulations and Python-based statistical methods to evaluate launch conditions and quantify landing-risk behavior under varying wind conditions.

### Rocketry Club & Launch-System Development
Founded a university rocketry organization and coordinated its first rocket project. Supporting engineering work included launch-system construction, CAD, 3D-printed components, and rocket assembly.
